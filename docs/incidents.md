# Incidents

To open an incident, you can either:

- Email support directly at [support@trmregistry.com](mailto:support@trmregistry.com)
- Open a GitHub issue

    Make sure to follow the [Contributing guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md#finding-the-right-project)

## Priority support

If you [upgrade your plan to PRO](https://trmregistry.com/plans), one of the benefits is priority support and direct contact with the maintainers ([Regesta Group Srl](https://www.regestaitalia.eu/)).

Contact sales for more details.
